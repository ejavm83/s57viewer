TIDAL CURRENTS
In the Eastward of Namhae Bridge, the E-going stream occurs approximately from 3hrs before HW of Yeosu Hang to 1hr after HW. When the tidal stream becomes the strongest, it is about 3.1kn in HW. The W-going stream occurs approximately from 2hrs after HW to 1hr after LW. When the tidal stream becomes the strongest, it is about 3.3kn in 1hr before LW.
