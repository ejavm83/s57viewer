Traffic Prohibited Area for Tankers
All tankers that carry any single material mentioned below are prohibited to navigate in the Traffic Prohibited Area except emergency situation, entry into and departure for ports. (Refer to the Article 14 of the Maritime Safety Act)
1. crude oil, heavy oil, or more than 1,500 kiloliter of diesel fuel
2. more than 1,500 kiloliter of oil designated in the Act of the Ministry of Oceans and Fisheries(MOF) complying with materials above (1), and among hydrocarbon oil, fake petroleum product and petroleum, substitute fuel described in the Article 2 of the Petroleum and Petroleum Substitute Fuel Business Act
3. more than 1,500 tons of noxious liquid material described in the Article 2 of the Marine Environment Protection Act
